Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

1. Who we are

Nima's Math Garden is a YouTube channel with short educational cartoons about math for children aged 3–5, published in nine languages. It is run by an independent creator. The cartoons, music and this website are made by us; the voices are synthesized.

2. This website

This website is a static page hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not use cookies, analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, forms or third-party scripts, and we do not collect any personal information through it. GitHub, as the host, may process technical data such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the site, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

3. Our YouTube channel

Our videos are hosted by YouTube and marked as "made for kids". When you watch them, YouTube's terms and privacy policy apply: YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy. Comments are turned off on our videos. We do not collect personal information from children or other viewers. YouTube only shows us aggregated, anonymous statistics (for example, the number of views).

4. FirstChann uploader (our use of YouTube API Services)

"FirstChann uploader" is a small internal tool that uses YouTube API Services. It is used only by the channel owner, on the owner's own computer, to upload our own videos, thumbnails and subtitles to our own channel, to add them to our playlists and to keep the channel's title and description translations up to date. It is not offered to the public and is not used to access anyone else's account.

  • What it accesses: only our own YouTube channel, after the channel owner signs in with Google and grants permission. It does not read or store data about viewers or other YouTube users.
  • How data is stored: the sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computer. The tool keeps a local list of which of our videos were uploaded and their YouTube IDs. Nothing is sent anywhere except to YouTube.
  • Sharing: we do not sell, share or transfer any data obtained through YouTube API Services.
  • Revoking access: access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page; the stored token then stops working and is deleted by us.
  • Deleting data: the tool stores no data about anyone except our own channel. If you want any data that we hold to be deleted, contact us (see below) and we will delete it within 7 days. Data obtained through YouTube API Services is never kept longer than needed to manage our own channel.
  • Cookies: neither the tool nor this website stores or reads cookies or similar technologies on your device.

By using YouTube through our tool you are bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Children's privacy

Our content is made for young children, and we take their privacy seriously. We do not knowingly collect, use or share personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent by email at konstatasn@gmail.com.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
